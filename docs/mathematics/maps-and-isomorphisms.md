## Abstract

These notes develop injective, surjective, and bijective maps together with inverse maps, homomorphisms, isomorphisms, homeomorphisms, and diffeomorphisms. They emphasize rigorous proof methods and apply the main ideas to the identification of vectors in \(\mathbb{R}^n\) with point derivations.
