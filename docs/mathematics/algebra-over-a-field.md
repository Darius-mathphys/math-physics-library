## Abstract

These notes develop the definition of an associative algebra over a field (K), starting with binary operations and progressing through groups, rings, fields, vector spaces, and bilinear maps. They explain how the algebra axioms fit together and use examples and nonexamples to clarify the role of associative, bilinear multiplication.
