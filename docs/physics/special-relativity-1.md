## Abstract

These notes develop the special-relativistic foundations used in cosmology.
They begin with Newtonian spacetime and Galilean transformations, introduce
the Galilei group and the postulates of special relativity, and then develop
affine spaces, vectors, tensors, Minkowski geometry, Lorentz and Poincaré
transformations, rapidity, four-momentum, and the transformation of
relativistic fields.
