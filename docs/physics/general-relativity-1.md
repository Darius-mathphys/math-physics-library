## Abstract

These notes introduce the differential-geometric foundations of general
relativity. They begin with the passage from affine spacetime to smooth
manifolds, then develop charts, tangent and cotangent spaces, vector and
tensor fields, connections and parallel transport, differential forms,
the exterior derivative, pullbacks, flows and Lie derivatives, and finally
metrics and Lorentzian geometry.
