## Abstract

These notes develop the metric geometry required for general relativity. They
derive the Levi-Civita connection from metric compatibility and vanishing
torsion, formulate parallel transport and the Riemann curvature tensor, and
compare flat and curved spherical geometries. They then introduce the Ricci
tensor, scalar and Weyl curvature, orthonormal frames, spin connections,
covariant exterior differentiation, and Cartan’s structure equations.
